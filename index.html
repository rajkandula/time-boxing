            How timeboxing works

            1. Brain dump. Write down everything you need to study, so it stops circling in your head.
            2. Pick your top 3. Star the tasks that matter most. Do those first.
            3. Box them. Give each task a fixed block of time and a clear goal.
            4. Focus. Work on one box until the timer ends. When time is up, stop and take a break.
            5. Review. Note how it went, then plan tomorrow’s boxes with what you learned.
